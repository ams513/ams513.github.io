Keep every word safe, and yours

5 features

  • Plain files you own

    Each chapter is an ordinary text file in your Documents folder.

    More detail about Plain files you own
    • Your book lives in Documents\My_Books, one file per chapter, named in reading order, such as 01 Prologue.md.
    • Any text editor can open it, today or in twenty years. If you stop using Quill, your book is still there.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Saves and backups, automatically

    Quill saves as you type and backs up the whole book.

    More detail about Saves and backups, automatically
    • A backup of the book at most every 2 minutes. The newest 25 are kept; older ones thin out to one a day, then one a week.
    • Preview and restore any backup from the Backups panel. Deleted chapters wait there too.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Paragraph graveyard

    Every paragraph you delete goes somewhere you can get it back.

    More detail about Paragraph graveyard
    • Deleted paragraphs are kept in Deleted_Graveyard.md in the book folder.
    • Put one back from the Graveyard panel with a click.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Zip backup to USB

    Save the whole book as one checked zip, on a USB stick or card.

    More detail about Zip backup to USB
    • Chapters, book details, notes, research, dictionary and exports in one file.
    • Quill reads the zip back and checks every file before it says it is done.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Nothing uploaded

    Quill runs on your computer and never sends your book anywhere.

    More detail about Nothing uploaded
    • The Library is the only part that goes online, and it is off until you turn it on.
    • Updates are checked only if you say yes, and each update is checked against a signature first.
    • In Free and Pro.

Shape the book

5 features

  • Chapters that behave

    Rename, reorder and delete chapters; the order stays in the file names.

    More detail about Chapters that behave
    • Drag a chapter, or use Move up and Move down.
    • A deleted chapter waits in Backups until you want it back.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Corkboard view

    One card per chapter, with a note and a status.

    More detail about Corkboard view
    • See the shape of the whole book, then jump into any chapter.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Search and replace

    Find any word across the whole book, and replace it everywhere.

    More detail about Search and replace
    • Jump straight to each match. Quill makes a backup before it replaces anything.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Lore and continuity checks

    Quill lists your people, places and dates, and flags slips.

    More detail about Lore and continuity checks
    • It flags a character who speaks after their death, dates that run backwards, ages that do not add up, and one event given two different years.
    • It only reads; it never edits your text. The checks are hints and can be wrong.
    • In Free and Pro.

  • Spell check for your names

    Spelling slips are underlined, but your invented names are left alone.

    More detail about Spell check for your names
    • Uses the spell checker built into Windows, on your computer, with no internet.
    • Names that Lore finds go into the book's own dictionary.txt, one word per line.
    • In Free and Pro.

Requirements and limits

Requirements

  • Windows: the full download includes Python; nothing else to install
  • macOS / Linux / small zip: Python 3.10 or newer

Honest limits

  • When a Pro 12-month key ends, Quill switches to Free. Your books stay readable, editable and exportable as text.
  • One key works on 2 devices. Keys make sharing hard, not impossible; there is no DRM that punishes buyers.
  • New release: there are no reviews yet, and this page shows none.

After you buy

Keys are issued by the seller and sent to the email address you used at checkout.

  1. Install: unzip the Windows full download and double-click install_quill.bat (no admin rights needed).
  2. Pro keys start with GQ1. Open Quill, click Free plan at the bottom left, paste the key and press Activate. Or double-click ACTIVATE_PRO.bat in the Quill folder and paste it there. No Python needed.
  3. A key works on 2 computers. To move it, open Licence on the old computer and press Turn Pro off on this PC, or write to support.
  4. PRO, 12 months from itch.io needs no key: the download is already activated, and the 12 months start the first time you open it.
  5. The key is checked offline. The device check is computed on your PC and never sent anywhere.

Stark Quill help: install, keys, updates, backups, common errors and refunds

Novel writing software that works offline, in plain files

Stark Quill is offline novel writing software. Every chapter is an ordinary text file in Documents\My_Books, named in reading order, so any editor can open your book today or in twenty years. Quill runs on your own computer, opens in your browser and does not upload anything.

It saves while you type, keeps a backup of the whole book every couple of minutes, and puts every deleted paragraph in a graveyard you can pull it back from. It goes online for only two things, the update check and the Library, and both are off until you turn them on.

The Free plan covers writing, chapters, backups, the graveyard and export to text, Markdown and a web page. Pro is $39 for 12 months, nothing renews by itself, and it adds EPUB, Word and print-ready PDF export. When the year ends, Quill switches to Free and your books stay yours.

If you want novel writing software with no AI: the optional AI notes in Pro run on your own PC through Ollama and only ask continuity questions. They never write, rewrite or continue your text.

Does Stark Quill work offline?

Yes. Quill works offline with no account. It goes online only for the update check and the Library, and both are off until you turn them on.

Is there a free version?

Yes. The Free plan covers writing, chapters, backups, the paragraph graveyard and txt, md and html export.

Does the AI write my book?

No. The optional AI notes run on your own PC through Ollama and only ask questions about continuity. They never write, rewrite or continue your text.

Where are my books stored?

In Documents\My_Books, one plain text file per chapter, with backups in the book's .backups folder.

What happens when the Pro year ends?

Quill switches to Free. Your books stay readable, editable and exportable as text, and nothing renews or charges you again.
