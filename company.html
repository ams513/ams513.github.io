About Stark

Software that keeps your work on your own computers.

Stark makes local-first software that helps people build, write and care for others on their own computers, with their work kept in their own hands. Our flagship is Stark Covenant: clinic records that stay on the clinic's own machines.

Our flagship

Stark Covenant

Clinic records kept on your own computers. Covenant opens in the browser on the clinic computer, so charts load from the local disk even when the internet drops.

Coming soon

  • Your records stay on your own computers

    Records, documents and backups are encrypted by the operating system, and the vault opens only with two of three keys: the PC, a USB stick and a recovery code.

  • You can see who wrote what, and when

    A note stays a draft until its author signs it with a PIN, and then it locks. Every view, sign-in and change goes into an audit trail that shows tampering.

  • Pay once and keep your records

    Each licence is paid once: no subscription and no setup fees. If you stop using Stark Covenant, your records stay readable and exportable.

How we build

The same rules apply to every product we make. Each one is something you can check in the downloads or in the page source.

  • Local-first

    Your work lives on your own computer. Stark Quill keeps every chapter as a plain file in Documents\My_Books, and Stark Covenant keeps records on the clinic's own machines.

  • Private

    These pages set no cookies and load no analytics or third-party scripts. The one exception is the checkout page, which loads Paddle's script when you open it to pay.

  • Works offline

    Stark World Engine runs offline on your computer, with no account. Covenant charts load from the local disk when the internet drops.

  • One file

    A game made in Stark World Engine exports as one HTML5 file that opens in any modern browser. Nothing else to install for the people who play it.

  • Honest pricing

    Prices are listed in full on one page. A Buy button appears only where you can actually buy, and plans without a checkout say so.

  • Refunds without a reason

    Stark Quill: a full refund if you ask within 7 days of purchase. Stark World Engine: within 2 days. The first purchase of a paid Covenant plan: within 7 days. See the Refund Policy.
