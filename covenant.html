Keep records on your own computers

4 features

  • Records on your own PC

    Everything stays on the clinic computer and opens in its browser.

    More detail about Records on your own PC
    • Covers patients, encounters, notes, vitals, allergies, medications, problems and documents.
    • It opens in the browser on that computer, so charts load from the local disk even when the internet drops.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Encrypted vault, three keys

    Records open only with two of three keys: PC, USB stick, recovery code.

    More detail about Encrypted vault, three keys
    • Records, documents and backups are sealed with the operating system's AES-256-GCM.
    • The recovery code is shown once, to print or save. The computer alone opens nothing.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Locks itself when idle

    The app locks after 10 minutes without use, and on request.

    More detail about Locks itself when idle
    • A Lock button is always on screen. The idle time is a setting.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Demo mode with sample patients

    Try every screen with synthetic patients before any real data.

    More detail about Demo mode with sample patients
    • Demo mode uses a separate folder with synthetic patients only, under a red banner on every screen.
    • Real encrypted records are never opened or changed in demo mode.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

Know who wrote what, and when

4 features

  • Signed notes that lock

    A note stays a draft until its author signs it with a PIN.

    More detail about Signed notes that lock
    • After signing, the note is locked. Anything learned later goes into an addendum, signed the same way.
    • The chart checks each signature again every time it opens.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Nothing quietly overwritten

    Vitals are never edited, and every change keeps the old version.

    More detail about Nothing quietly overwritten
    • A wrong vital is marked "entered in error" with a reason and stays visible.
    • Allergy, medication and problem entries keep each earlier version; nothing is deleted.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Audit trail with integrity check

    Every view, sign-in and change is logged, and tampering shows.

    More detail about Audit trail with integrity check
    • The log is append-only and hash-chained, and holds ids, never names or note text.
    • Verify integrity finds edited, added, removed or reordered entries, and changed record files.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Suggestions need sign-off

    Helper suggestions reach a chart only after clinician sign-off.

    More detail about Suggestions need sign-off
    • A suggestion goes into the clinician's own draft only after they accept it with their PIN, and the draft still needs signing.
    • Signed notes cannot take suggestions.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

Never lose a day of work

2 features

  • Safe saves if power fails

    Each save is journaled, so a power cut leaves no half-written chart.

    More detail about Safe saves if power fails
    • At the next start, an unfinished save is finished or discarded, so a chart is as it was or as it was saved.
    • This holds as long as the disk honours write flushes.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Encrypted automatic backups

    Backups run on their own, and the newest 20 are kept.

    More detail about Encrypted automatic backups
    • At start-up, every 30 minutes after changes, and on demand.
    • A backup restores into a new, empty folder, so a restore never overwrites what you have.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

Give each person the right access

3 features

  • Roles for the whole teamIn Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian

    Clinician, nurse, front desk and admin each see only their part.

    More detail about Roles for the whole team
    • Front desk registers and checks in patients but cannot open clinical notes. Admins manage accounts and backups and see no clinical data.
    • Nurses read medications and problems without changing them.

  • Careful sign-in

    Each person has their own login, with lockout after wrong tries.

    More detail about Careful sign-in
    • Five failed logins lock the account for 15 minutes, and the lock survives a restart.
    • Signing has its own PIN, separate from the password.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Use across the clinic networkIn Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian

    Other computers in the clinic can reach it, when you turn this on.

    More detail about Use across the clinic network
    • Off by default. It is plain HTTP, so use it only on a trusted network.

Share records with other systems

1 feature

  • HL7 v2 and FHIR R4

    Export a patient's record in HL7 v2 or FHIR R4; the toolkit reads both.

    More detail about HL7 v2 and FHIR R4
    • One patient becomes a FHIR R4 transaction Bundle and an HL7 v2.5.1 ADT^A08 message.
    • Checked for structure, not by the official HL7 validator.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

Get help fast in an emergency

1 feature

  • Emergency button on every screen

    Shows the country's emergency numbers as big call buttons, no sign-in.

    More detail about Emergency button on every screen
    • On every screen, including sign-in and while the vault is locked. A person always presses the button; nothing calls by itself.
    • It does not replace calling your local emergency number directly, and a call may not connect (no signal, no credit, no network).
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

Optional helpers for the front office

4 features

  • Research desk with citations

    Search medical literature and get cited drafts for the clinician to judge.

    More detail about Research desk with citations
    • Searches sources such as PubMed, Europe PMC, WHO and ClinicalTrials.gov. Results are drafts with citations and quoted passages.
    • Patient identifiers are stripped before a search leaves the computer; Offline mode sends nothing.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Insurance paperwork help

    Track claims and deadlines, and draft letters a person checks and sends.

    More detail about Insurance paperwork help
    • Plan comparison worksheets, a claim tracker with appeal deadlines, and draft prior-authorisation and appeal letters.
    • It never sells, recommends or enrols anyone in a plan.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Consent per purpose

    Patients and staff opt in to each purpose, and can withdraw any time.

    More detail about Consent per purpose
    • Every purpose is off until the person says yes. Withdrawing takes effect at once.
    • Each consent gets a signed receipt.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

  • Review kit for your lawyer

    Checklists and templates to bring to your own compliance review.

    More detail about Review kit for your lawyer
    • Jurisdiction checklists for the US, EU/UK, Canada, Australia, India and Brazil.
    • Templates: privacy notice, patient and staff consent forms, a DPIA, a breach runbook, a DPA and a BAA.
    • In Baby, Solo, Practice, Clinic and Humanitarian.

Offline electronic health records for a small clinic: what Stark Covenant does

Stark Covenant is not a certified EHR or medical device; your compliance review is required before real patient data. No HIPAA or GDPR compliance is claimed. Read that first if you are comparing records software for a small clinic.

What it does: local-first electronic health records for small practices. It runs on your own machines and the records stay there, with no cloud dependency. Records are encrypted through the operating system's own crypto (Windows CNG), and HL7 v2 and FHIR R4 import and export tools move data in and out.

Suggestions only count after a clinician signs off. It does not diagnose. Each licence is paid once, with no subscription and no setup fees, and if you stop using Stark Covenant your records stay readable and exportable.

Plans follow the size of the practice. Solo is one clinician on one user account. Practice covers two to four users and Clinic five to ten users, each at one flat price with multi-user roles. Baby is a free solo demo for one person. Every plan runs the same uncertified software.

Does Stark Covenant need the cloud?

No. It runs on your own machines and records stay local, with no cloud dependency.

Can a small clinic with several staff use it?

Yes. The Practice plan covers two to four users and the Clinic plan five to ten users, each at one flat price with multi-user roles. Solo is one clinician on one user account.

Can I put real patient data in it today?

Only after your own compliance review (privacy law, security and clinical governance where you work). It is not certified as an EHR or as a medical device.

Can it exchange records with other systems?

It has HL7 v2 and FHIR R4 import and export tools.

Is it a subscription?

No. Each licence is paid once: no subscription and no setup fees. If you stop using Stark Covenant, your records stay readable and exportable.
