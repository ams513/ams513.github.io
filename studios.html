Get a playable game in minutes

3 features

  • Make me a game

    Pick a theme and a size, and get a complete game to play.

    More detail about Make me a game
    • Six themes, from Spooky Swamp to Cozy Village, and three sizes: Snack (1 level, about 15 minutes), Evening (3 levels, about an hour) and Epic (6 levels, a few hours).
    • The game has villages, people with quests, monsters, chests, keys, locked doors, portals and a boss.
    • Type a seed to share: the same seed and theme always make the same game.

  • Three starter games

    Open a finished game, play it, then change anything you like.

    More detail about Three starter games
    • Firefly Fen (3D, 3 levels), Summit Snack Run (3D, 2 levels) and Lantern Leap (2D, 3 levels).
    • Two simple templates as well: Classic Crystal Hunt and Classic Platformer.

  • Setup check and tour

    A short tour on first visit, and plain help when setup goes wrong.

    More detail about Setup check and tour
    • Before it starts, the launcher checks for common first-install problems, such as running from inside the zip or a folder it cannot save to, and says how to fix each problem.
    • Press ? and then Fix my setup to run the same check at any time.

Build worlds by clicking

6 features

  • 3D world builder

    Pick a tool, click the ground, and your world fills up.

    More detail about 3D world builder
    • Place trees, rocks, ruins, houses, lamps, crystals, people, chests, locked doors, portals and checkpoints.
    • A level holds up to 5,000 objects. Drag to turn the camera; WASD moves it.

  • 2D platformer levels

    Paint tiles to make side-scrolling levels with spikes, water and bosses.

    More detail about 2D platformer levels
    • Tiles: ground, pearls, spikes, water, flag and exit. Creatures: slime, archer and boss.
    • Draw your own hero in the Pixel Art tab and use it as the player.

  • Moods, weather, day and night

    One click changes the sky, light, fog and floating particles.

    More detail about Moods, weather, day and night
    • Five Living World Moods, such as Bioluminescent Twilight and Sub-Zero Aurora.
    • Per level: rain, thunderstorm, snow and other weather; a day that lasts 10 minutes, 5 minutes or 90 seconds; lakes, hills and world size.

  • Bring your own 3D models

    Drop in your own models, textures and skies, then place them.

    More detail about Bring your own 3D models
    • Accepts .glb (or .gltf with its files), PBR textures (.png / .jpg) and .hdr skies in the Assets tab.
    • Files that need Draco or meshopt compression are refused with a note: export them again without compression.

  • Autosave, undo and backups

    Your work saves itself, and older versions wait if you need them.

    More detail about Autosave, undo and backups
    • Autosave runs about 15 seconds after your last change. Undo and redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
    • Every save keeps the three most recent older versions; Restore an earlier version brings one back.

  • 8 interface languages

    The main buttons and tabs come in 8 languages.

    More detail about 8 interface languages
    • English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese and Chinese. The studio remembers your pick.
    • The tool palette, editor panels and in-game text stay in English.

Write a real adventure

7 features

  • Quests in one click

    Add a ready-made quest, then change its steps and reward.

    More detail about Quests in one click
    • Five starting types: Fetch, Hunt, Talk, Explore and Escort.
    • Steps can be collect items, defeat enemies, talk to someone, go to a place or escort someone. Rewards are XP, gold and an item.
    • A gold ! marks people with a quest, and a gold beam points to the next goal.

  • Dialogue with answers

    Write what people say, with answers that change the story.

    More detail about Dialogue with answers
    • Each line can have up to 8 answers. An answer can end the talk, go to another line, start or finish a quest, or give or take an item.
    • Rules pick the first line from quest progress or from what the player has.

  • Items and equipment

    Make potions, keys, treasure and equipment that makes the player stronger.

    More detail about Items and equipment
    • Item kinds: quest item, potion or food, key, equipment (weapon, armour, boots or charm) and treasure.
    • Equipment adds attack, defence or max HP. Players open the bag with I and drink a potion with H.

  • Enemies and bosses

    Five kinds of enemy, each fighting in its own way.

    More detail about Enemies and bosses
    • Slime chases, Archer shoots from a distance, Brute smashes the ground, Wisp floats fast, and a Boss has a health bar and special attacks.
    • Hits use hit-stop and screen-shake, so they feel punchy.

  • XP and new abilities

    Players level up and earn Dash, Whirl and Star Bolt.

    More detail about XP and new abilities
    • Defeating enemies and finishing quests gives XP. Dash comes at level 2, Whirl at level 3 and the Star Bolt at level 4.
    • Falling in battle sends the player back to the last checkpoint and costs 10% of their gold.

  • Games with many levels

    Link up to 25 levels, 3D and 2D in one game.

    More detail about Games with many levels
    • Portals, flags and exits lead on. A portal can stay closed until a quest is done.
    • XP, items and gold carry over from level to level.

  • Save slots for players

    Players can stop, close the browser and carry on later.

    More detail about Save slots for players
    • Three save slots, plus an autosave at every checkpoint.
    • Players pick Easy, Normal or Hard in the Esc menu.

Make it look and sound like yours

6 features

  • Lit 3D scenes

    Live light, shadows and water, drawn right in your browser.

    More detail about Lit 3D scenes
    • PBR materials, HDRI skies, soft sun shadows, fog that glows toward the sun, swaying grass, and water with reflections and shore foam.
    • Graphics presets Low, Medium, High and Auto keep older laptops smooth.

  • Art library included

    Characters, houses, trees and skies made by artists, ready to use.

    More detail about Art library included
    • glTF models from Quaternius and skies and textures from Poly Haven, all released as CC0.
    • Every export lists the art it uses, so crediting is done for you.

  • Pixel-Art Canvas

    Draw a 16 x 16 sprite and make it your 2D hero.

    More detail about Pixel-Art Canvas
    • Paint with a palette, then press Use as player sprite.
    • Save PNG writes a crisp 128 x 128 picture into your game folder.

  • SFX Forge

    Shape retro sound effects with sliders, no audio software needed.

    More detail about SFX Forge
    • 12 presets, from jump and coin to portal and magic, and four wave shapes.
    • Save WAV keeps the file and makes it the sound for that action in your game.

  • Soundtrack maker

    Make an original looping tune that matches the mood of your level.

    More detail about Soundtrack maker
    • About 40 seconds long, composed on your computer, and yours to use.
    • It plays in the studio and is saved as a WAV in your game folder; the HTML5 file uses sound effects only.

  • Drone camera and postcards

    A slow cinematic camera flight over your world, for screenshots and trailers.

    More detail about Drone camera and postcards
    • Press P for a cinematic flight with letterbox bars.
    • Postcard PNG saves the current view; in an exported game, O does the same.

Share your game as one file

    Your whole game in one .html file that opens in a browser.

    More detail about One-file HTML5 export

Keep building in Godot or Unreal

3 features

  • Godot 4 project exportIn Pro and Studio

    Open your level in Godot 4 as a lit starting scene.

    More detail about Godot 4 project export
    • 3D levels get a sky, global light, fog, glow, hilly ground with collision and a walking third-person camera. 2D levels become a tile scene with a player.
    • Objects come in as simple shapes you can swap for real art. Needs Godot 4.2 or newer; we tested it in Godot 4.7.
    • It builds the first level. Quests, dialogue and combat travel along in world.json but are not re-created in Godot.

  • Unreal Engine 5 export (preview)In Pro and Studio

    Turn a 3D level into an Unreal Engine 5 starting project.

    More detail about Unreal Engine 5 export (preview)
    • Lumen, Nanite, Virtual Shadow Maps, a sky matched to your mood, fog and colour grading, plus a Stark menu that builds the level from your data.
    • Swap placeholders for your own Fab or Megascans assets with one menu item. Needs Unreal Engine 5.4 or newer.
    • A preview: we checked the files, but have not yet run this export in a real Unreal Engine 5 install. 3D levels only; first level only.

  • Example HTML5 gamesIn Pro and Studio

    The starter games as ready HTML5 files, to play and learn from.

    More detail about Example HTML5 games
    • Pro and Studio downloads include example cartridges of the starter games.

Film and publish videos about your game

5 features

  • Setup WizardOnly in Studio

    A checklist in your browser sets up the video tools in about 5 minutes.

    More detail about Setup Wizard
    • Checks Python, FFmpeg, OBS and a browser, and can run the official installers for you.
    • Tests your camera and microphone, links your phone with a QR code, and records a 5-second test clip.

  • OBS scene writerOnly in Studio

    Sets up an OBS profile and scene for recording, in one step.

    More detail about OBS scene writer
    • Writes a new profile and scene with camera, phone, screen and microphone. Recordings land where the editor finds them.
    • Your existing OBS profiles and scenes are never changed; anything it touches is backed up first.

  • Automatic video editorOnly in Studio

    Drop in a recording, get an edited video and three vertical clips.

    More detail about Automatic video editor
    • Makes FINAL.mp4 plus three 1080 x 1920 clips for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.
    • Trims silences, evens out loudness, adds captions, colour, gentle zooms and transitions, then opens a review page.

  • Phone hub with PINOnly in Studio

    Plan shoots, read a teleprompter and review edits from your phone.

    More detail about Phone hub with PIN
    • Tabs: Shoot Scheduler, Camera, Teleprompter, Review & Approve, and Stats.
    • Protected by a 6-digit PIN. Five wrong PINs in 10 minutes lock that device out for 15 minutes. Use it on your home Wi-Fi only.

  • Publishing helpersOnly in Studio

    Upload to your YouTube channel and get post drafts for other sites.

    More detail about Publishing helpers
    • YouTube videos upload as private, to your channel, with your own Google credentials.
    • For X, Instagram and Substack it writes the post text and opens the compose page. You press Post; nothing posts by itself.

Requirements and limits

Requirements

  • Windows 10/11: nothing to install, Python is included
  • macOS / Linux: Python 3.10 or newer
  • A WebGL2 browser: current Chrome, Edge or Firefox
  • Godot export: Godot 4.2+. UE5 export: Unreal Engine 5.4+
  • Studio creator suite: Windows 10/11, FFmpeg, OBS Studio 30+

Honest limits

  • A creative and fast-prototyping tool, not a replacement for a full engine.
  • Godot and UE5 exports are a starting scene of your first level; they do not re-create the gameplay.
  • UE5 export: the generated project was checked, but not yet run in a real Unreal Engine 5 install at release.
  • Works without a key. A key registers your copy and turns on the Pro and Studio exporters. Keys are checked offline.
  • New release: there are no reviews yet, and this page shows none.

After you buy

Keys are issued by the seller and sent to the email address you used at checkout.

  1. After buying you receive one line of text that starts with GS1- (your key).
  2. Start the studio (START_GORGON_STUDIOS.bat on Windows), open Export > Activate, paste the key and press Activate.
  3. Or save the key as key.txt next to studio_app.py; the studio reads it when it starts.
  4. No internet or account is needed: the key is checked on your computer. A missing or mistyped key never stops the program; it shows "unregistered" and keeps running.

Questions

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. Stark World Engine Free is a free download on itch.io: the whole builder, including 3D and 2D worlds, Make me a game, moods and drone mode. Its HTML5 export includes the first level of your game, with a small closable "Made with Stark World Engine" note. Pro and Studio export every level with no note.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Everything runs on your computer and the studio listens on 127.0.0.1 only. There is no account and no subscription; paid editions are paid once, and keys are checked offline.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10/11 with nothing to install (Python is included), or macOS / Linux with Python 3.10 or newer. You also need a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox for WebGL2.

Can I import my own 3D models?

Yes, since 28.5. Open the Assets tab and drop in a .glb (or a .gltf with its files), PBR textures or an .hdr sky. Files that need Draco or meshopt compression are refused: export them again without compression.

Can I sell the games I make?

The games you make are yours. Do not resell or redistribute Stark World Engine itself.

Which edition should I get?

Start with Free to build, play and share the first level. Pro to share whole HTML5 games and to continue in Godot 4 or Unreal Engine 5. Studio if you also make videos about your games.

How do I pay?

Stark World Engine and Stark Quill are sold on itch.io. Stark Covenant plans will be paid for through Paddle, our merchant of record, once card checkout opens. Either way, this site never sees or stores your card details.

How do I get my key?

Keys are issued by the seller and sent to the email address you used at checkout. The thank-you page explains how to activate each product.

Does this page track me?

No. These pages set no cookies and load no analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts or fonts. The one exception is the checkout page: once card checkout through Paddle is open, that page loads Paddle's checkout script when you open it, so Paddle can take the payment. No other page loads it.
